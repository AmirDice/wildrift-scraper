Hecarim at a glance

Hecarim is GOD tier across EU and NA with a top-50-main win rate of 57.7%. The best tracked main on either server peaks at 85.0%.

  • Warpath turns 12% bonus move speed into attack damage, so move-speed items carry offensive value.
  • Rampage is spammable, which makes spellblade and ability haste efficient and keeps Phase Rush up.
  • Spirit of Dread heals off damage dealt and bonus health, so resistances and bonus HP compound.

Kit identity

Damage & scaling

Physical damage, scaling with AD, bonus AD and AP.

On-hitMobility HealingCrowd control

Build around

Move speed, ability haste and spellblade. Everything in the kit converts tempo into damage.

Kit synergies

  • Passive Warpath converts 12% bonus move speed into AD, so Trinity Force, Black Cleaver and Celerity all add damage on top of their stats.
  • Q Rampage is a short-cooldown spammable ability, which makes spellblade and ability haste highly efficient and keeps Phase Rush rolling.
  • W Spirit of Dread heals from damage dealt and from bonus health, so healing amplification and resistances compound rather than add.
